Warm surfaces. Useful controls. An assistant that asks before acting. AsterVale explores how a familiar phone can feel considered again.

Personal, at your pace

A soft palette, your favorite shortcuts and a choice of light or dark. Explore every screen in the browser workbench.

Performance you can understand

Per-app profiles, clear trade-offs and thermal protection. Hardware tuning remains off until the A51 can be measured.

Your data stays your decision

The prototype stays in your browser. The Android assistant starts disabled. Cloud inference is still under development.

Open source.
Open about progress.

The first release is source and design work. There is no flashable AsterVale ZIP yet. We’re building on the Exynos9611 community’s Android 16 work, one reviewable change at a time.
